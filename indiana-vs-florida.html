Indiana vs Florida — Surprising Affordability

Indiana is one of the most affordable states in the entire country with housing costs well below the national average. Despite having a 3.15% income tax Indiana's overall cost of living is significantly lower than Florida making it a surprisingly strong competitor on pure affordability grounds.

Why Hoosiers Move to Florida

Indiana winters can be harsh with significant snowfall and cold temperatures. Florida's warm weather and zero income tax are major draws especially for retirees. Many Indiana residents become snowbirds spending winters in Florida before eventually making a permanent move to escape the Midwest winters.

Best Florida Cities for Indiana Residents

Many Indiana residents choose the Gulf Coast for its affordability and relaxed atmosphere. Fort Myers and Cape Coral are popular choices offering lower prices than Miami or Tampa. Orlando is also popular with Indiana families for its theme parks and growing job market.
