Maryland vs Florida — The DC Suburb Escape

Maryland is one of the wealthiest states in America due to its proximity to Washington DC but that wealth comes with a high cost. Maryland's 5.75% income tax combined with high housing costs especially in Montgomery and Prince George's counties make Florida an attractive alternative for many Maryland residents seeking to reduce their tax burden.

Government Workers and Remote Employees

Maryland has a large population of federal government workers and contractors. With the rise of remote work many of these high earners are discovering they can keep their Washington DC salaries while living in Florida and paying zero state income tax. This represents a significant financial windfall especially for senior government employees.

Best Florida Cities for Maryland Residents

Many Maryland residents choose the Jacksonville area for its East Coast feel and proximity. The Space Coast and Daytona Beach areas are also popular. Tampa offers a vibrant city atmosphere similar to the Baltimore Washington corridor but at a much lower cost of living.
