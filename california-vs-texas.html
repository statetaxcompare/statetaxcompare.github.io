Why People Move from California to Texas

California has one of the highest state income tax rates in the country at 13.3%, while Texas has zero state income tax. For someone earning $100,000 a year, that alone saves over $8,000 annually. Combined with lower rent, cheaper gas and groceries, the total savings can exceed $13,000 per year.

Things to Consider

While Texas has no income tax, it does have higher property taxes than California. The average effective property tax rate in Texas is around 1.6% compared to 0.7% in California. If you own an expensive home, factor this into your calculations.

Most Affordable Texas Cities

San Antonio and El Paso offer the lowest cost of living in Texas. Austin and Dallas are more expensive but still significantly cheaper than Los Angeles or San Francisco.
