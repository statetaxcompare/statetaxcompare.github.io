Why New Jersey Residents Are Fleeing to Florida

New Jersey has the highest property taxes in the entire United States and a top income tax rate of 10.75%. It is no surprise that thousands of New Jersey residents move to Florida every year. Florida offers zero income tax, no inheritance tax, and significantly lower property taxes.

The Retirement Factor

Florida is especially attractive for New Jersey retirees. Social Security income is not taxed in Florida, pension income is not taxed, and the warm weather is a major draw after decades of New Jersey winters. Many retirees find they can live very comfortably in Florida on the same income that felt tight in New Jersey.

Best Florida Cities for New Jersey Residents

Many New Jersey transplants settle in the Tampa Bay area or on the Gulf Coast for its calmer waters and lower prices compared to Miami. Boca Raton and Fort Lauderdale attract those who want to stay close to the energy of a major metro area.
