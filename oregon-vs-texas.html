Oregon vs Texas — Pacific Northwest vs Lone Star State

Oregon has one of the highest income tax rates in the country at 9.9% with no sales tax to offset it. Texas has zero income tax but does have sales tax. For high earners the income tax difference is enormous. Moving from Oregon to Texas can save over $6,500 per year in total cost of living with even greater savings for those in higher income brackets.

Portland vs Austin — A Tale of Two Cities

Portland and Austin are both known for their vibrant music scenes outdoor culture and progressive attitudes. However Austin has zero state income tax while Portland residents pay nearly 10% to Oregon. Austin's tech boom has made it one of the fastest growing cities in America attracting many Oregon residents seeking lower taxes and warmer weather.

Best Texas Cities for Oregon Residents

Austin is the natural fit for Portland and Eugene residents seeking a similar creative outdoor culture. San Antonio offers a more affordable alternative with excellent food culture and a strong military presence. Dallas attracts Oregon professionals in finance and corporate sectors with excellent career opportunities.
