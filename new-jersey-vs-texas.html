New Jersey vs Texas — The Garden State Exodus

New Jersey has the highest property taxes in the United States and a top income tax rate of 10.75%. Combined with very high housing costs New Jersey residents pay some of the highest overall tax burdens in the country. Texas offers zero state income tax significantly lower housing costs and a booming economy making it one of the most popular destinations for New Jersey residents.

The Financial Case for Moving

A New Jersey family earning $200,000 per year pays approximately $21,500 in state income tax. Moving to Texas eliminates this entirely. Combined with lower rent and cheaper daily expenses the total annual savings can exceed $30,000 for a typical upper middle class family. This represents a life-changing financial improvement.

Best Texas Cities for New Jersey Residents

Dallas is the most popular destination for New Jersey transplants offering a major metropolitan experience with zero state income tax. The Dallas suburbs of Frisco, Plano, and Allen offer excellent schools and a high quality of life. Houston and Austin are also popular choices with strong job markets in energy, tech, and healthcare.
