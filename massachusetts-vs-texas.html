Massachusetts vs Texas — Boston vs The Lone Star State

Massachusetts is one of the most expensive states in America with a 9% income tax and Boston housing costs among the highest in the nation. Texas offers zero state income tax and housing costs that are dramatically lower than Boston. Moving from Massachusetts to Texas can save over $14,000 per year making it one of the most financially rewarding relocations on the East Coast.

The Tech Worker Calculation

Boston is a world class tech and biotech hub. However those high salaries are taxed at 9% by Massachusetts. Remote tech workers are discovering they can keep their Boston salary while living in Austin or Dallas and paying zero state income tax. For someone earning $200,000 this represents $18,000 per year in additional take-home pay.

Best Texas Cities for Massachusetts Residents

Austin is the most popular destination for Boston tech workers with a similar innovative startup culture and university atmosphere. Dallas offers a more traditional corporate environment with excellent infrastructure. Houston attracts those in energy and medical fields with world class hospitals and research institutions.
