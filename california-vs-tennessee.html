California vs Tennessee — The Nashville Boom

Tennessee has zero state income tax and some of the lowest housing costs among major metropolitan areas in the United States. Nashville has exploded in popularity becoming one of the fastest growing cities in America. Moving from California to Tennessee can save over $16,000 per year making it one of the most financially rewarding relocations possible.

Nashville vs Los Angeles

Nashville offers a vibrant music and arts scene growing tech industry and outstanding food culture at a fraction of the cost of Los Angeles. Major companies including Oracle and Amazon have relocated significant operations to Nashville. For California tech workers the combination of zero income tax and affordable housing makes Tennessee extremely compelling.

Other Great Tennessee Cities

While Nashville gets most of the attention Chattanooga and Knoxville offer excellent quality of life at even lower costs. Memphis is another option with a rich music heritage and very affordable housing. All Tennessee cities benefit from the zero state income tax making them financially attractive compared to California.
