Why People Move from New York to Florida

New York has a 10.9% state income tax plus New York City adds another 3.876% if you live there. Florida has zero state income tax. On a $100,000 salary, moving from NYC to Florida saves over $14,000 per year in taxes and cost of living combined.

Things to Consider

Florida has no state income tax but does have higher homeowners insurance costs due to hurricane risk. Property insurance in Florida can cost 2-3x more than in New York. Factor this into your calculations if you plan to buy a home.

Most Popular Florida Destinations for New Yorkers

Miami, Tampa, and Orlando are the most popular destinations. Many New Yorkers also choose smaller cities like Sarasota or Naples for a quieter lifestyle with lower costs.
