Georgia vs Florida — Two Southern States Compared

Georgia is actually more affordable than Florida overall despite having a 5.49% income tax. Atlanta's housing costs are lower than most major Florida cities and Georgia's cost of living index is below the national average. For budget-conscious residents Georgia often wins on pure affordability.

Why Georgians Move to Florida

Florida's zero income tax is a major draw for high-earning Georgia residents. On a $150,000 salary moving from Georgia to Florida saves over $8,000 per year in state income tax alone. Florida's beaches and year-round warm weather are also major attractions for Georgia residents seeking a lifestyle upgrade.

Best Florida Cities for Georgia Residents

Jacksonville is the closest major Florida city to Georgia and is very popular with Atlanta residents looking to relocate. Tampa and Orlando offer strong job markets and are within easy driving distance of Georgia. Many Georgia retirees also choose the Gulf Coast for its affordability and warm weather.
