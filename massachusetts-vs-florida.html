Massachusetts vs Florida — The Boston to Miami Move

Massachusetts is one of the most expensive states in the country with a 9% income tax and some of the highest housing costs in the Northeast. Florida offers zero income tax and significantly lower rent making it an increasingly popular destination for Massachusetts residents tired of high costs and brutal winters.

The Tech Worker Calculation

Boston is a major tech and biotech hub with high salaries. However those same high salaries are taxed at 9% by Massachusetts. Many remote tech workers are discovering they can keep their Boston salary while living in Florida and paying zero state income tax — a financial win of tens of thousands per year.

Best Florida Cities for Massachusetts Residents

Miami attracts many Boston professionals for its cosmopolitan energy and nightlife. However many Massachusetts families prefer the calmer Gulf Coast cities like Sarasota or Naples. Orlando is also popular for its family-friendly environment and lower cost of living.
